# ⚡ Quick Start Guide - Video Quality Selection

## 🚀 Ready to Use in 3 Steps!

### Step 1️⃣: Play a Video
Search for and play any video in the Dopamine app.

### Step 2️⃣: Open Quality Selector
Tap the **⚙️ settings icon** in the top-right corner (it shows your current quality).

### Step 3️⃣: Choose Your Quality
Select any quality from the list:
- **2160p (4K)** 🔴 - Ultra HD
- **1440p (2K)** 🟠 - Quad HD
- **1080p** 🔵 - Full HD ⭐ (default)
- **720p** 🟢 - HD
- **480p** 🟠 - SD
- **360p** ⚪ - Low

That's it! Your video will continue playing at the new quality. 🎉

## 🎯 Quick Tips

### 💡 Which Quality Should I Choose?

| Your Situation | Recommended Quality |
|----------------|-------------------|
| 🖥️ Large 4K TV/Monitor | **2160p (4K)** |
| 💻 Desktop/Laptop | **1080p** or **1440p (2K)** |
| 📱 Phone/Tablet | **720p** or **1080p** |
| 🐌 Slow Internet | **480p** or **360p** |
| 📊 Limited Data Plan | **480p** |
| 🎵 Audio Focus | **360p** |

- `Q` - Open quality selector

- `Esc` - Close selector

### 🔍 Finding Quality Info
- Settings icon shows current quality (e.g., "1080")
- Quality dialog shows all available options
- Color badges indicate quality level
- "Currently playing" shows active quality

## 📋 Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: "I want maximum quality"
```
1. Open quality selector
2. Select the TOP option (highest available)
3. Usually 2160p (4K) or 1440p (2K)
4. Enjoy crystal-clear video!
```

### Scenario 2: "Video is buffering"
```
1. Open quality selector
2. Select LOWER quality (e.g., 720p or 480p)
3. Smoother playback with less buffering
4. Can increase later when connection improves
```

### Scenario 3: "Saving mobile data"
```
1. Open quality selector
2. Select 480p or 360p
3. Much less data usage
4. Still watchable quality
```

### Scenario 4: "Background listening"
```
1. Play video
2. Select lowest quality (360p)
3. Or switch to audio-only mode (🎵 icon)
4. Minimal data, focus on sound
```

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Does changing quality restart the video?
**A:** No! The video continues from the exact same position.

### Q: Are all qualities available for every video?
**A:** No, it depends on the video. Some older videos may not have 4K.

### Q: What's the default quality?
**A:** 1080p (Full HD) for the best balance of quality and performance.

### Q: Can I switch quality during playback?
**A:** Yes! You can change quality anytime without interrupting playback.

### Q: Why is 4K not showing for some videos?
**A:** Not all videos are uploaded in 4K. The app shows only available qualities.

### Q: Does higher quality use more data?
**A:** Yes! 4K uses much more data than 720p. Choose based on your data plan.

### Q: Can I set a preferred quality?
**A:** Currently, it defaults to 1080p. Quality preference saving is a future feature.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### Problem: Quality selector won't open
**Solution:** 
- Ensure video is playing
- Try closing and reopening the app
- Check internet connection

### Problem: Quality change failed
**Solution:**
- Check your internet connection
- Try a lower quality first
- Wait a few seconds and try again

### Problem: Video buffering after quality change
**Solution:**
- This is normal for a few seconds
- If it continues, select a lower quality
- Check your internet speed

### Problem: 4K option not showing
**Solution:**
- This is normal - not all videos have 4K
- Try searching for "4K" videos specifically
- Check if video source supports 4K

## 🎯 Best Practices

### ✅ DO:
- Start with 1080p (default)
- Lower quality if buffering occurs
- Use lower quality on mobile data
- Try 4K on fast WiFi with large screen

### ❌ DON'T:
- Select 4K on slow connections
- Use high quality with limited data
- Rapidly switch between qualities
- Force high quality if buffering
