# Video Quality Selection - User Guide

## 🎬 How to Change Video Quality

### Step 1: Access Quality Settings
While watching a video, look for the **Settings icon (⚙️)** in the top-right corner of the video player. This icon displays your current quality (e.g., "1080", "720").

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ←  🏠        [Video Playing]    ⚙️  │  ← Click this!
│                                1080  │  ← Current quality
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 2: View Available Qualities
A beautiful quality selector dialog will appear showing all available qualities for the current video:

```

║  ╰─────────────────────────────────╯  ║
║                                       ║
║  ╭─────────────────────────────────╮  ║
```

### Step 3: Select Your Preferred Quality
- Tap any quality option to switch
- A loading indicator will appear
- Video continues from the same position
- Success message confirms the change

## 📊 Quality Levels Explained

| Resolution | Badge | Description | Use Case |
|------------|-------|-------------|----------|
| **2160p (4K)** | 🔴 4K ULTRA HD | Highest quality, very sharp | Large 4K TVs, high-end monitors |
| **1440p (2K)** | 🟠 2K QHD | Excellent quality | High-resolution displays |
| **1080p** | 🔵 FULL HD | Great quality (default) | Most modern displays |
| **720p** | 🟢 HD | Good quality | Standard HD displays |
| **480p** | 🟠 SD | Moderate quality | Slower connections |
| **360p** | ⚪ Low | Basic quality | Very slow connections |

## 💡 Tips & Recommendations

### When to Use Each Quality:

#### 🌟 **4K (2160p)** - Best
- ✅ Fast internet (25+ Mbps)
- ✅ 4K display/TV
- ✅ Unlimited data plan
- ❌ Avoid on mobile data

#### ⭐ **1080p (Full HD)** - Recommended Default
- ✅ Good internet (10+ Mbps)
- ✅ Most displays
- ✅ Best balance of quality and data
- ✅ Default selection

#### ⚡ **720p (HD)** - Balanced
- ✅ Moderate internet (5+ Mbps)
- ✅ Smaller screens
- ✅ Mobile devices
- ✅ Saves data

#### 📱 **480p/360p** - Data Saver
- ✅ Slow internet
- ✅ Limited data plans
- ✅ Background playback
- ✅ Audio focus

## 🎯 Features

### ✨ Smart Quality Selection
- **Automatic Detection**: App finds all available qualities for each video
- **Best Match**: Selects closest available quality if exact match isn't available
- **Seamless Switch**: Change quality without losing your position
- **Position Memory**: Continues from exact spot when switching

### 🎨 Visual Indicators
- **Current Quality Badge**: Shows on settings icon (e.g., "1080")
- **Color Coding**: Each quality level has a unique color
- **Selection Marker**: Green checkmark shows current quality
- **Status Text**: "Currently playing" vs "Tap to switch"

### ⚡ Performance
- **No Buffering**: Pre-loads new quality before switching
- **Instant Switch**: Minimal interruption to playback
- **Smart Caching**: Remembers quality selection during session

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Quality Not Available?
Some videos may not support all quality levels. The app will show only available qualities for that specific video.

### Quality Switch Failed?
- Check your internet connection
- Try a lower quality first
- Restart the app if issue persists

### Video Buffering After Quality Change?
- This is normal for a few seconds
- Higher qualities need more bandwidth
- Consider using a lower quality if buffering continues

## 📖 Additional Features

### From Audio Player
You can also access quality settings from the audio player screen when preparing to switch to video mode.

### Quality Persistence
Your selected quality stays active for the current playback session.
